Student performance predictor

A machine learning tool that estimates a student's exam score from their study habits, lifestyle, and background — and shows which factors actually move the needle.

Try the predictor

The problem

Teachers and students often rely on intuition to guess what's holding a score back — more studying, better attendance, tutoring. This project tests that intuition against real data: which factors actually predict outcomes, and by how much?

The dataset

Trained on Student Performance Factors, a dataset of 6,607 students from Kaggle, covering 19 factors across four areas: study habits (hours studied, attendance, tutoring), lifestyle (sleep, physical activity, extracurriculars), family background (parental involvement, income, education), and school environment (resources, teacher quality, distance from home).

How it predicts

A linear regression model — chosen after comparing it against a random forest on held-out data — learns a weight for each of the 19 factors and combines them into a single predicted score. In testing, its predictions landed within about half a point of the real exam score on average.
